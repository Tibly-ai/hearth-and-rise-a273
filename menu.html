From Grain to Table

The 36-Hour Sourdough Process

No commercial yeast, no additives. Only pure flour, filtered water, grey sea salt, and a decades-old wild starter culture.

01

Milling & Autolyse

We source high-extraction grains from small family farms, mill them cool, and let flour hydrate naturally before adding salt or starter.

02

Fold & Ferment

Instead of mechanically kneading, we gently fold the wet dough by hand every thirty minutes, building natural gluten structures.

03

The Cold Cure

Shaped loaves rest in traditional wicker baskets inside a cold chamber for 24 hours. This slowly coaxes complex lactic and acetic acids to form.

04

Hearth Baking

Baked directly on heavy firestone decks with high pressure steam, resulting in a thick, blistered, amber crust and soft interior.
