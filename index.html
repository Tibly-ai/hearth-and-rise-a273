◇ Handmade Sourdough Process ◇

Slide to witness the 36-hour transformation from simple water and flour to crisp wild crumb.

Three honest ingredients.
Thirty-six quiet hours.

At Hearth & Rise Sourdough, we believe that bread is a living connection to local soil. We refuse to speed up the process with industrial additives or chemical yeast.

Instead, we rely entirely on natural wild cultures and long cold-proofing periods. This slow work coaxes rich lactic and acetic acids out of the grains, resulting in a robust, deeply caramelized crust, a tender open crumb, and natural digestibility.
